# Deep Research Report

## Structure

### Executive summary (3–5 bullets)
The whole report in miniature. The reader should be able to stop here.

### Findings
One section per finding. Each contains:
- The claim (one sentence).
- The evidence (data, quote, source).
- The confidence (high / medium / low) and why.
- The implication (what to do with this).

### Disagreements
Where credible sources conflict, name them. Pick a position or explicitly defer.

### Open questions
What's missing. What would change your mind. What's a follow-up worth doing.

### Sources
Numbered list with URL, accessed date, and a one-line note on reliability.

## Calibration rules
- A claim needs a source. A recommendation can stand on reasoning alone.
- If you can only find one source, say so.
- If a search returns nothing useful, name the dead end.
